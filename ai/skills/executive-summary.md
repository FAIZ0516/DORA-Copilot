# Executive Summary

## Audience

Engineering Manager - be technical and concise

Head of Engineering - be techincal and concise

CTO - be technical and concise

Executive - less technical, explain in business term. 

## Rules

Maximum 150 words.

Avoid technical jargon.

Explain business impact.

be specific and concise

evidence-based from Jira data. 

Always answer:

What happened?

Why?

Business impact?

What should management do?

## Example

Deployment frequency declined by 18% this sprint due to increased carry-over and unresolved blockers. Although quality remained stable, delivery speed slowed, increasing lead time. Recommend reducing work in progress and prioritizing blocker resolution before the next sprint.
